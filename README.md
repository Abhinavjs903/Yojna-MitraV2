# 🌟 YojnaMitra


YojnaMitra is a student-focused financial assistance platform designed to simplify access to scholarships, internships, government schemes, financial policies, and personal expense management.

Built with the vision that **financial limitations should not stop student dreams**, YojnaMitra brings important resources together into one easy-to-use dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🎓 Scholarship Discovery

- Explore government scholarship opportunities
- Filter scholarships based on:
  - Education level
  - Scholarship type
  - Student category
- Redirect students to official application portals

---

### 💼 Internship Finder

Helps students discover verified opportunities based on:

- Current academic year
- Domain interests
- Career goals

Supported areas:

- Technical internships
- Policy & governance
- Finance
- Social impact

---

### 💰 Expense Tracker

A personal money management assistant for students.

Features:

- Track monthly income
- Add expenses
- Calculate savings
- Analyse spending habits
- Provide personalised financial suggestions

---

### 🏦 Financial Policies Hub

Provides information about:

- Education loans
- Interest subsidy schemes
- Tax benefits
- Startup support programs

---

### 🗣️ Student Voice

A space where students can share financial challenges related to:

- Scholarships
- Loans
- Hostel expenses
- Bills
- Daily expenses
